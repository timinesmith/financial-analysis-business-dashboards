# Financial Reporting Methods and Controls

**Status:** Illustrative demonstration.

## Monthly workflow

1. Lock the reporting calendar and name preparer/reviewer roles.
2. Import or enter budget, actual, commitment, and forecast data.
3. Reconcile totals to source reports before analysis begins.
4. Calculate dollar and percentage variances using consistent formulas.
5. Require explanations for material variances and unusual trends.
6. Separate one-time items from recurring performance.
7. Record corrective actions, owners, and due dates.
8. Archive the issued version so later changes are traceable.

## Control principles

- No unexplained plug figures.
- No overwritten source data; adjustments are separate lines.
- Reviewer approval is documented for settlements and material journal corrections.
- Customer, employee, and banking details are excluded from portfolio examples.
- Forecasts are scenarios, not guarantees, and assumptions must be visible.
